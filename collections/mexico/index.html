Mexico Culture

This collection is a single product line — a matte, biodegradable wheat-straw case — carrying four Mexican pattern stories. Each motif is a same-colour 3D relief pressed into the shell, not a print: it never fades or peels, and the surface reads as texture rather than decoration. Built for retailers and gift buyers serving Mexico and the wider Latin American market who want cultural depth with an eco material story.

#DYF2452 · Biodegradable · Laser-Engraved Aztec Geometry Biodegradable Case

The pattern collects four building blocks of Aztec and Maya design — the stepped pyramid as a sacred mountain, the sun stone as the turning of time, the stepped-fret spiral as movement and the union of opposites, and the diamond lattice as an ordered cosmos and its four directions. Read together: cosmic order, the link between earth and sky, and a civilisation that has lasted.
#DYF2453 · Biodegradable · Laser-Engraved Alebrijes Art Biodegradable Case

The alebrije is a Mexican folk-art invention: a fantastical creature built from many animals at once — feline body, wings, horns, a curling serpent tail — covered in dense scrollwork. Born from a craftsman's fever dream in 1930s Mexico City and later carved in Oaxaca, it's read as a spirit guide between worlds, and a celebration of imagination itself.
#DYF2454 · Biodegradable · Laser-Engraved Floral Embroidery Biodegradable Case

The motif comes from Mexican hand embroidery — the scrolling flowers of Oaxacan blouses and the flower-and-bird work of Otomí Tenango textiles — with a hummingbird in flight at its centre. Flowers stand for abundance and pride; the colibrí, in Nahua belief, is a messenger of joy and love; the stitching itself, for patience handed down.
#DYF2455 · Biodegradable · Laser-Engraved Mayan Glyphs Biodegradable Case

The pattern sets Maya hieroglyph cartouches against Kukulkan, the feathered serpent that descends the staircase of El Castillo at the equinox. The glyphs stand for knowledge carved to outlast its makers; the serpent, joining earth and sky, for renewal and the turning of cycles.
