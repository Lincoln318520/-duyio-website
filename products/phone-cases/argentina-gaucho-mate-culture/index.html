The Design

The line-art figure on this case is a gaucho — the horseback herders of Argentina's Pampas plains, historically tied to freedom, self-reliance and a deep connection to the land. She's drawn mid-gesture, holding a mate gourd and its metal bombilla straw.

Sharing mate — Argentina's national drink, brewed in that same gourd and passed hand to hand between friends, family, even strangers — is one of the country's most enduring social rituals, standing for hospitality and trust more than the drink itself. The case renders it in fine gold linework over a clear, glitter-flecked resin base, finished with an electroplated camera frame.

It's a design built around a cultural moment rather than a team or a flag, which makes it a natural fit for gift shops, cultural retailers and Latin American-focused sellers looking for something with a story behind it, not just a national color scheme.
