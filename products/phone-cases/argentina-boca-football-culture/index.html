The Design

Deep blue and bright yellow, cut in hard-edged blocks — this is what's known in Argentina as the “Boca colors,” the palette that fills La Boca, the Buenos Aires neighborhood home to Boca Juniors, one of the country's most storied football clubs. It's a color combination people recognize on sight, long before they'd call it a jersey.

Scattered through the case, small gold-foil fragments catch light like flecks of sunshine — a direct nod to the Sol de Mayo, the sun at the center of Argentina's flag, symbolizing freedom, dawn and new beginnings. It's a quieter reference sitting inside a loud color story.

This design works for the same kind of buyer as our other Argentina pieces: retailers and gifting businesses serving football culture and Latin American diaspora communities, where the color story alone does the identifying — no crest or logo needed.
