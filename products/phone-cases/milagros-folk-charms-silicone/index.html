The Design

Milagros are the small votive charms left at church altars across Mexico — hearts, eyes, hands and doves offered in thanks or in hope of protection and blessing. This case arranges four of the most recognisable forms, sacred heart, protective eye, open hand and dove, each with fine radiating linework, so the phone case itself reads as a charm carried every day.

The pattern is laser-engraved directly into the shell in its own colour, not printed, holding as a fine tonal texture that won't fade, scratch off or peel and stays completely smooth to the touch. A soft fleece lining protects the phone's back panel and camera, and the matte, soft-touch silicone gives a cushioned, anti-slip grip.

Built for buyers who want a design with genuine emotional pull rather than pure decoration — available in six colourways and cut for the full iPhone 11 to 18 range.
