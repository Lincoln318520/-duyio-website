The Design

This design comes straight from the emblem at the centre of the Mexican flag: an eagle perched on a nopal cactus, a serpent in its beak, drawn from the Aztec founding legend of Tenochtitlan. It's the most direct expression of Mexican identity in the collection — detailed feather texture across the wings, fine thorn detail on the cactus paddle, and a heroic, dynamic pose.

The pattern is laser-engraved directly into the shell in its own colour rather than printed, holding as a fine tonal texture that won't fade, scratch off or peel and staying completely smooth to the touch. A soft fleece lining protects the phone's back panel and camera, and the matte, soft-touch silicone gives a cushioned, anti-slip grip.

Built for buyers who want the single most recognisable, least-explaining-required Mexican symbol in their range — available in six colourways and cut for the full iPhone 11 to 18 range.
