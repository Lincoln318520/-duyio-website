The Design

A single number carries the weight of this design: the 10. In Argentine football, the No. 10 shirt has meant something beyond a squad number since Mario Kempes wore it to Argentina's first World Cup win — through Diego Maradona's most famous moments, to Lionel Messi finally lifting the trophy in 2022. This case renders that number as a raised, dimensional centerpiece, not a flat print.

The sky-blue and white base is Argentina's own palette, and the gold glitter running along the diagonal split and around the numeral does double duty: it echoes the shine of the World Cup trophy itself, and the three gold stars worn above Argentina's crest — one for each World Cup win.

It's built for a specific kind of buyer: someone whose customers wear this number, not just recognize it — football culture retail, fan merchandise, or Latin American gift shops looking for real specificity instead of generic team colors.
