The Design

Papel picado — Mexico's traditional perforated paper banner — shows up at festivals, weddings and Día de los Muertos altars across the country. This case translates its scalloped-edge banner shape and floral, bird and geometric cut-outs into pure linework: the same act of removing material to reveal a pattern that defines the paper craft is, here, what a laser does to a silicone shell.

The design is laser-engraved directly into the shell in its own colour, not printed — a fine tonal texture that won't fade, crack or peel, and reads as completely smooth under the fingertip. A soft fleece lining protects the phone's back panel and camera from scratches, and the matte, soft-touch silicone gives a cushioned, anti-slip grip.

Suited to buyers serving Mexico's festival, wedding and gift-shop trade, where the papel picado look already carries strong recognition — available in six colourways and cut for the full iPhone 11 to 18 range.
