The Design

Unlike our culture-driven pieces, this design doesn't point to a specific place — its meaning is carried entirely by color and material. Deep red petals sit in contrast against cooler blue blossoms, a pairing meant to read as warmth and calm held together at once, rather than one dominant mood.

Scattered silver star flecks break up the floral pattern with something less literal — a nod to holding onto a hope or a wish, even in an ordinary day. The petals themselves are real dried flowers, sealed permanently into the resin layer rather than printed — a way of preserving an actual, physical moment rather than illustrating one.

Because it isn't tied to a flag or a fandom, this piece works as a broader gifting or lifestyle item — floral and botanical retailers, gift shops, and any buyer whose customers respond to romantic, nature-driven design rather than cultural iconography.
