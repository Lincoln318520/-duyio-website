The Design

Barro negro — Oaxaca's hand-carved black-clay pottery — is one of Mexico's most important craft traditions, its surfaces typically carved with birds, florals and geometric swirls in a rustic, hand-worked style. This case takes that same bird-among-vines motif and gives it a bolder, simpler line than a Talavera tile pattern, adding textural range to the collection.

The pattern is laser-engraved directly into the shell in its own colour, not printed, so it holds as a fine tonal texture that won't fade, chip or peel and stays completely smooth to the touch. A soft fleece lining protects the phone's back panel and camera, and the matte, soft-touch silicone gives a cushioned, anti-slip grip.

A fit for buyers who want Mexican handmade craft represented with a more rustic, grounded character — available in six colourways and cut for the full iPhone 11 to 18 range.
